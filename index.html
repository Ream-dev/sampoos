Our signature daily shampoo infused with hydrolyzed silk proteins gently cleanses while deeply nourishing every strand. Sulfate-free, dermatologist tested, and suitable for all hair types.

✓ Sulfate-Free ✓ Paraben-Free ✓ Vegan ✓ Cruelty-Free ✓ Dermatologist Tested ✓ Eco-Friendly Packaging
🚚 Free shipping over $50 ↩ 30-day returns 🔒 Secure checkout

Natural Silk Daily Shampoo is the cornerstone of our luxury hair care collection. Formulated with patented Hydra-Silk Complex™ and enriched with pure botanical extracts, this daily cleanser gently removes impurities while infusing each strand with vital nutrients.

The unique blend of hydrolyzed silk proteins works at a molecular level to fill gaps in the hair cuticle, creating a smooth surface that reflects light for unparalleled shine. Unlike conventional shampoos, our sulfate-free formula preserves your hair's natural oils and color vibrancy.

Suitable for daily use on all hair types including color-treated, chemically processed, and naturally fine hair. Clinically proven to reduce breakage by 89% and increase shine by 3.2x in independent trials.

We believe in full transparency. Every ingredient is ethically sourced, sustainably produced, and rigorously tested for safety and efficacy.

Key Active Ingredients:

Hydrolyzed Silk Protein Argan Oil Keratin Complex Aloe Vera Green Tea Extract Biotin Vitamin E Provitamin B5 Chamomile Extract Jojoba Oil Caffeine Hyaluronic Acid

Free from: Sulfates · Parabens · Phthalates · Artificial Colors · Mineral Oil · Formaldehyde

For best results, follow this simple routine:

Step 1. Wet hair thoroughly with warm water. Warm water opens the cuticle for deeper penetration of silk proteins.

Step 2. Apply a generous amount (about the size of a quarter for short hair, more for longer hair) to your palms and distribute evenly through hair from roots to tips.

Step 3. Massage gently into the scalp using your fingertips in circular motions for 2–3 minutes. This stimulates circulation and maximizes silk protein absorption.

Step 4. Leave on for 1–2 minutes for extra conditioning benefits.

Step 5. Rinse thoroughly with cool water to seal the cuticle and lock in shine. For best results, follow with Natural Silk Conditioner.
